Bug Bounty Program

Help us secure our platform. We reward security researchers who disclose vulnerabilities responsibly.

🎯 Scope Definition

✅ In Scope

  • Main web application (`*.example.com`)
  • Public APIs and endpoints
  • Authentication and Authorization flows
  • Business logic vulnerabilities

❌ Out of Scope

  • Social engineering / Phishing
  • Physical attacks on infrastructure
  • Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS)
  • Automated scanning without consent

💰 Severity & Rewards

Rewards are based on the impact of the vulnerability as determined by our security team.

Severity Example Vulnerabilities Reward
Low UI bugs, Info leak, Missing headers $50
Medium Stored XSS, Auth bypass, CSRF $200
High SQLi, Privilege escalation, SSRF $500
Critical RCE, Full takeover, Database dump $1,000+

RULES & GUIDELINES

Learn Today. Lead Tomorrow

⚡
Never attempt to modify, destroy, or access data belonging to other users.
⚡
Do not disrupt our production systems or degrade user experience.
⚡
Provide clear, step-by-step instructions to reproduce the vulnerability.
⚡
Wait for our security team to resolve the issue before disclosing it publicly.
